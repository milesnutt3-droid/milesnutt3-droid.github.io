Take theirs. They get yours.

A party game for a room full of people and their phones.

Download NowiPhone · free · no accounts

How a round works

  1. Answer. Everyone answers the same question in secret.
  2. Switch. One player takes somebody else's answer, or keeps their own — without knowing whose is whose.
  3. Vote. Every answer appears with no names. The room votes.

Whoever ends up holding the winner takes the crown. You find out who wrote what at the very end. Full rules →

Playing together

  • Three to nine players, a phone each or one passed around.
  • Phones talk over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. No internet needed.
  • No accounts, no servers, no ads.
