What Nabbed is

Nabbed is a party game for people in the same room. Everyone answers the same question, one player may swap answers with somebody else, and the room votes. The app supplies the questions. The players supply everything else.

Moderation

Beyond that, the room is responsible for its own tone. Nabbed is designed for a private group who chose to play together, and it does not connect you to strangers.

If someone writes something objectionable:

  • Tap Report on the answer. It is hidden on your screen and the host is notified.

  • You can leave the room at any time.
